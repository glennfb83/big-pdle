BIG PDLE

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. New puzzle every time!

Green = right letter, right spot · Yellow = in the word · Gray = not in the word

How to play

Guess the secret five-letter word in six tries. After each guess, the colors show how close you are:

  • Green: right letter in the right spot.
  • Yellow: letter is in the word, but in another spot.
  • Gray: letter is not in the word.

Type with your keyboard or tap the letters. Big Pdle is unlimited—start another puzzle whenever you like.
